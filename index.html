Quick Facts

Annotation reliability 92.3% initial agreement Multi-annotator workflow with review
Annotation granularity 4 levels Detection, localization mask, explanation, quality score
Localization difficulty >85% defects <5% area Most defects are tiny and localized

Key Points For Review

What is new

  • Unified benchmark for defect detection, localization, explanation, and quality scoring.
  • Covers both open-source and proprietary image generators.
  • Designed with multi-annotator verification for reliability.

Why it matters

  • Most defects are small and localized, making this task harder than coarse AGI quality scoring.
  • Failure cases show current models still miss text artifacts and common-sense contradictions.
  • The dataset is directly useful for both benchmarking and supervised fine-tuning.
